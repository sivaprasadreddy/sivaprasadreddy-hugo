Afternoon thoughts

Life changed after COVID

No maids, do everything by self

Nostalgia kicked in

I grew up in a small village, not very much interested in further studies, never imagined myself becoming a software engineer

No confidence in myself, fear of public speaking

Finally got a job with a friend's recommendation

Used to wait at ATM for salary credited message. If credited go to Bavrchi, if not go to curries point.

Used to feel so jelous of those software engineers who work at big IT companies and going to office by office cab/bus.

Got ourselves into troubles by showing off like a newly married husband

You never know how life turns out.
The rock stars in college failed to adjust in Corporate world.
The back bencher is enjoying his life in USA.

Curd - Yogurt
Rupees - Bucks

BTW, if u think everyone settled in abroad is enjoying their life you are wrong.

My way of interviewing changed drastically. It's very hard for newbies to get into programming thesedays.

Even experienced people
Kneel down before Webpack,
Git please

Be thankful that you have job, you have plenty of online resources to learn for free

Get at least some people care about you.

Life is supposed to be enjoyed slowly, without rushing.
